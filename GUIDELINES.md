# Guidelines

This is a self-directed exercise. There is no walkthrough, no starter notebook
and no list of steps.

## Format

- Plan on paper first. Raise your hand and talk a reviewer through your plan
  before you open your laptop.
- Use any tools you like, including AI tools. You are responsible for every
  claim in your write-up and must be able to defend it in person.
- If something is ambiguous, make a reasonable assumption, state it, and move on.

## How we will evaluate you

- Judgment: why this problem, and why not the others
- Data intuition: what you noticed about this specific data
- Rigor: whether your claims hold up when we probe them
- Scoping: what you included, what you cut, and why
- Honesty about limits: what the data cannot support

There is no single right answer. Well-justified choices, including ones we
might not have made, are what we want to see.

## Logistics

Take the time you need. Submit your write-up, your code or notebook, and any
charts or tables it references.
