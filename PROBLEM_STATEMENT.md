# Problem Statement

## The situation

A fleet customer operates the vehicles in this dataset. They have told us they
want to **reduce the cost of running their fleet**, and they have asked us what
we would build for them first.

## What you have

One week of real, anonymized telemetry in `data/raw/`: speed, acceleration,
fuel level, oil life, odometer, engine RPM, gear position and ignition status,
plus make/model/year for each vehicle. There is no location data and no VIN.

The data is not clean and is not handed to you as one table. Understanding what
it is, and what it can and cannot tell you, is part of the exercise.

## What we want from you

Using only this data, decide **what we should build first** for this customer,
and show that the data supports it.

You choose the problem. You define what success looks like and how we would
measure it. You work out which signals could carry it, and where they are
unreliable. You decide what is in a first version and what is out.

## Deliverable

A short write-up (aim for 2-3 pages) containing:

- The problem you chose, and why you chose it over the alternatives you considered
- The success metric, and how it would be computed from this data
- Evidence from this dataset that the idea is feasible: your own numbers, charts
  and at least one thing that surprised you
- What this data cannot support, and what you would need to ask for or collect
- What is in version one and what you are cutting
- Your riskiest assumption, and how you would test it

Include whatever analysis you did to get there. We care about how you got to the
answer at least as much as the answer.

You will walk a Motorq reviewer through this in person.
